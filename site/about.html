About Nana K IT Solutions Consult

A practical IT consulting business focused on dependable technology, clearer information, and better-supported operations.

Business introduction

Technology support built around real business needs

Nana K IT Solutions Consult provides IT services and consulting for small and growing organizations. The business supports clients with troubleshooting, maintenance, networking, management information systems, basic cybersecurity controls, and system administration.

The approach is consultative: understand the operational problem first, then select and implement a technology solution that is proportionate, maintainable, and clearly documented.
