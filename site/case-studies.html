Networking

Small Business Network Setup

Problem

Unreliable wireless coverage and unmanaged connectivity were affecting staff productivity.

Approach

Reviewed the physical layout, device count, user needs, existing cabling, and security requirements before defining a practical network design.

Solution

Implemented a structured wired and wireless design with managed switching, secure access, guest segmentation, and documented configuration.

Results

Improved consistency, simplified troubleshooting, and created a clearer path for future network expansion.

IT Support

IT Support for a Law Firm

Problem

Recurring workstation and account problems were handled informally, resulting in avoidable downtime.

Approach

Grouped recurring issues, prioritized critical user functions, introduced maintenance routines, and documented support steps.

Solution

Standardized support triage, software updates, endpoint checks, account administration, and user guidance.

Results

Reduced repeated interruptions and gave staff a more predictable way to obtain assistance.

MIS

Management Information System for a Clinic

Problem

Operational data was distributed across paper records and inconsistent spreadsheets, slowing routine reporting.

Approach

Mapped the information flow, identified essential data fields, clarified ownership, and designed a lightweight digital workflow.

Solution

Created structured data capture, basic role-based handling, and management reports supported by a database-backed workflow.

Results

Improved information consistency and made routine operational reporting faster and easier to review.

IT Support

Office 365 & Email Setup

Problem

The organization needed professional email, consistent user setup, and stronger sign-in practices.

Approach

Reviewed domain ownership, user requirements, mailbox structure, DNS, device access, and security basics.

Solution

Configured Microsoft 365 settings, business mailboxes, DNS, MFA guidance, and repeatable staff onboarding steps.

Results

Provided a professional email environment with clearer account ownership and improved sign-in security.
