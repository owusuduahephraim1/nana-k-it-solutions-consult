Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through this website and related Nana K IT Solutions Consult services is handled.

Information we collect

When you contact us, request a consultation, open a support ticket, subscribe to updates, use the client portal, or arrange remote support, we may collect information you provide such as your name, email address, telephone number, service request, support description, preferred consultation date, and messages exchanged with the support team.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, prepare consultations and quotations, provide technical support, maintain service records, operate the client portal, manage website content, protect the service from abuse, and improve business operations.

Infrastructure and service providers

The website is published through GitHub Pages. Application requests may be processed by Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare Turnstile. Application records are stored in Neon PostgreSQL. Managed files may be stored in Cloudflare R2. These providers process information as needed to deliver the service.

Security

Administrative and client records are protected by authenticated access controls. Public forms use security verification and submission rate limiting. Managed file administration is restricted, and file responses are configured to avoid persistent caching so deleted files become unavailable promptly.

Retention

Operational records are retained for as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, support history, business administration, security, and applicable record-keeping obligations. Records may be archived or deleted when no longer required.

Your choices

You may contact us to request correction of information you submitted, ask questions about our handling of your information, or request removal where appropriate. Newsletter subscriptions can be deactivated by the business upon request.
